### About You

I look for serious builders making economically meaningful projects. My focus is on “time to magic”: how quickly can we help you take your notion and turn it into knowledge, reputation and wealth generation in the market. Days not weeks. Hours not days.

My practice focuses on the “hardest 5%” of the project, which is why I operate on a coaching model. So much of the work of no-code can be managed by you. But there’s always the sticking point, either in conceptualization, planning, higher-dimensional thinking, or the process of actually shipping.

When you are at the hardest 5%, that’s where I want to help get you past it.

### About Ray

I am a 25-year veteran of software and data science. I have grown teams of technologists across generations of technologies as a founder, CTO, and consultant to Fortune 500 companies. You can learn more [about me](https://raydeck.com/) and my [track record](https://raydeck.com/testimonials). For the last two years, I have been connecting business domain experts more closely with low-code technology to automate processes, create tools to enhance their businesses, and convert previously bespoke services into equity-creating product offerings via apps.

### How I Work

Domain experts can make tremendous progress when they are empowered to create directly.

I work with you live. I am not in the business of creating offline, and you will always know how much time you’ve used because you were right there.

For anything implemented on your no-code system, you drive - I don’t take over your keyboard. That way we make sure you understand what is going on as we work, and you don’t have a “how did that happen?”

For situations that require a bit of code that you don’t know how to write, I will often whip something up and share it with you live. I use tools like [ScriptTag](https://scripttag.statechange.ai/) for this purpose so we can both see what is going on. We will take time to learn how that code works and why it might be necessary.

We will learn together. I take on hard problems that usually nobody or very few people have done before in the particular way that you need them. In this process, you will learn my diagnostic techniques and mental models that enable much faster progress beyond our sessions together.

### A Step Up: Why Private Coaching?

I find there are four good reasons to engage in private coaching:

You have a deeper challenge that requires more extensive work together. Stringing together a number of novel or complex elements. Teaching a deeper concept. There are hard topics that require just more time, and the accelerator program can be a good fit for breaking that out. But before we go down this road, drop me a line with the question first - something that seems hard for you might not be as hard as you think, and is a good fit for the pro community.

You are looking for more coaching in a shorter period of time than the slices in the pro group office hours and forum discussion support. If you join the pro group and want to go faster, we can do that! The idea behind the accelerator is to complement your experience with deeper connections to work through hard problems but also do more in a shorter number of days. Speed is momentum, and momentum leads to shipping. Let’s go!

You want to bring a group to the work. Private coaching doesn’t have to be 1-1. In fact, it is at its best when it is supporting those who support you. Bring your colleagues so we can make maximum progress.

You need to discuss sensitive issues that need to not be recorded or shared with others. Totally fine! We work together in confidence, and you can choose whether you want a recording of the work for your own records. Discussions that involve displaying secret keys or business planning both can fall into this category. Many product initiatives ties into the strategy of the enterprise, and discussing those issues allows better decisions that can cut cost and increase speed. This confidentiality is a great reason to engage in private coaching

### Intensive Low-Code Acceleration Program: 10 Hours, $8,000

Teams facing shipping a complex, data-intensive feature can get intensive coaching and technical insight to move quickly through their challenge.

Recent projects include setting up a multi-gigabyte data warehouse for reporting and an automated real estate valuation system.

The accelerator starts with daily hourly meetings for at least a week. We work on cracking open the problem rapidly, with homework at the end of the session. The objective is to have executed more than half of the initiative in this time.

After the initial daily sequence, your team is ready to run the ball and we hold less frequent meetings to unstick particular hard points. We complement this with email correspondence which can often clear up questions a lot earlier than waiting for the next Zoom call. State Change Pro members can use the forum and office hours there to get more insights that draw on the broader group’s wisdom without having to use the hours in this program, allowing even more speed.

We set a month as the outside boundaries of the engagement, but our objective is to get you cracked in the first week and shipped in the second. The faster we move, the higher your return.

This project should be the primary objective for the accelerator participants from your team.

Payment is due at the beginning of the program.
